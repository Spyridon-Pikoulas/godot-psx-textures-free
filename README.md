# PSX Textures Free

**16 free PS1-style textures for your retro 3D level**: brick, concrete, plaster, hospital paint
and tiles, floorboards, rusty steel and corrugated iron, grass, asphalt and ceiling tiles, with a
wainscot, a door, an exit sign and a blood splatter. Walls, floors and ceilings tile seamlessly.
Each one is a 16-colour, ordered-dithered PNG like the PS1's own, at 128 and 64 px, with a ready
Godot material. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX Textures](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-textures)** has 138: nine bricks, eleven
concretes and stones, wallpapers, fourteen tiles, twelve woods, carpets, nineteen metals (chain
link and grates see-through), ground, street, roofs, ceilings, doors, windows, eleven signs and
thirteen decals, and a Unity package. Same addon: it installs over this one.

### What's inside

- **Walls:** red brick, concrete, cracked plaster, hospital mint paint, dirty white wall tiles,
  rusty corrugated iron.
- **Floors:** lino tiles, floorboards, grass, asphalt, rusty steel plate.
- **Ceiling:** ceiling tiles.
- **Details:** a tiled wainscot for a wall's lower metre, a wooden door, an exit sign, a blood
  splatter with transparency.
- **Every texture at 128 px to the metre and again at 64**, 16 colours each (the PS1's 4-bit
  palette), the dither tiling with the texture.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a material per texture (point filtered, repeating, lit by your lights, the splatter
a cutout) and the demo room. Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns your level over in
one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.

### Any engine

The PNGs in folders by category, at 128 and 64 px. Set point filtering and turn mipmaps off.
